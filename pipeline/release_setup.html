Before you begin

  1. Install Scene Capture through Xcode with your Apple signing team, or use a published TestFlight link.
  2. Use a rigid chest mount. Prepare cameras and verify both previews plus LiDAR.
  3. Record a slow room sweep, including floor and upper walls, followed by interaction. Export the whole session through Files or Finder.
